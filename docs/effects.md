# Effect Catalog

## Natural

- `fire` — chaotic orange/red flame simulation
- `campfire` — slower, warmer fire movement
- `candle` — subtle warm flicker
- `storm` — dark blue ambience with irregular lightning
- `ocean` — slow blue/cyan breathing
- `aurora` — long green/cyan/purple transitions

## Energy / Sci-Fi

- `plasma` — chaotic blue/purple energy
- `reactor` — cyan core pulses with surges
- `tesla` — sharp electric blue flashes
- `portal` — cycling blue/purple portal motion
- `warp` — accelerating blue/white pulse sequence

## Aura / Cyber

- `blueaura`
- `redaura`
- `goldaura`
- `violetaura`
- `awakening` — escalating power-up into a persistent blue aura
- `cyber` — cyan/magenta/purple neon movement
- `synthwave` — purple/pink/orange transitions
- `matrix` — green digital flicker
- `glitch` — intentionally irregular color jumps

## Horror / Experimental

- `heartbeat` — double red heartbeat pulse
- `haunted` — dim eerie color changes with occasional flashes
- `failinglight` — unstable white-light simulation
- `randomwalk` — color drifts unpredictably through hue space
- `entropy` — starts orderly and gradually becomes chaotic

## After Dark

- `redroom` — deep crimson breathing
- `desire` — red/magenta irregular pulses
- `slowheat` — violet-to-red warming sequence
- `tease` — dark base with unpredictable brightness swells
- `build` — steadily increasing pulse intensity and speed
- `afterdark` — longer evolving low-light sequence

## Multi-Bulb

- `chase` — moves brightness across discovered bulbs
- `clash` — opposing blue/red lights with white collision flashes
- `crosspulse` — alternating red/purple pulses between two bulbs

Multi-bulb effects gracefully fall back to a single-bulb effect when only one bulb is available.

## Safety

Some effects include rapid brightness changes. Avoid strobe-like effects around anyone with photosensitive epilepsy or sensitivity to flashing lights.
